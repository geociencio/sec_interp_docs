sec_interp.gui.adapters.drillhole_extractor module

Drillhole extraction adapter (Extract phase).

Bridges the QGIS object world and the QGIS-agnostic core layer for drillholes. It reads the section line and collar layers, buffers the line, detaches collars, pre-samples collar elevations from a DEM, and fetches survey/interval data — returning a fully-detached DrillholeContext.
