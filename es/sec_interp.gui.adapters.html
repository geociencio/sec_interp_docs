sec_interp.gui.adapters package

GUI adapters (Extract phase).

Adapters bridge the QGIS object world and the QGIS-agnostic core layer. They perform the «Extract» step of the Extract-then-Compute pattern: resolving layers, reading features, transforming CRS, and buffering — everything that needs live QGIS objects — so the core never has to.
